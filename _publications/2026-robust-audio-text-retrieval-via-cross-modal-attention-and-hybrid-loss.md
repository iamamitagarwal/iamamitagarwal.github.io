**Abstract.** The paper develops an audio–text retrieval framework that refines both modalities through transformer projections, linear mappings and bidirectional attention. A combined cosine, L1 and contrastive training objective supports smaller batches, while silence-based segmentation and attention pooling address lengthy, noisy recordings. Benchmark experiments evaluate retrieval improvements over earlier approaches.
