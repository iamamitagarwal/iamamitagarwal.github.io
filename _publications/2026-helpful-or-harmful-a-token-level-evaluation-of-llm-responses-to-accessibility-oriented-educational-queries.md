**Summary.** This study assesses answers to accessibility-related teaching questions from 16 LLMs across nine disability contexts. A token-based judging framework analyzes relevance, support, answers, stigma and conflicts with accessibility needs. Helpful categories dominate the extracted tokens, while the authors caution that automated judgments of ethically sensitive content remain unreliable and recommend human oversight.
