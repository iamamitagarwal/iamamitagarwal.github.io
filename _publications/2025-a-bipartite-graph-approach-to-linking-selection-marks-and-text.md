**Abstract.** The paper formulates the association of document selection marks with their text labels as a bipartite graph matching problem. It combines OCR and mark recognition with an optimization step to connect detected marks to the relevant text. Evaluation across ten document categories reports a mean F1 score of 95.95%, supporting integration into document-processing pipelines.
