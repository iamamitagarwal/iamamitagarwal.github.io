<article class="case-study" id="knowledge-systems" aria-labelledby="knowledge-heading">
  <header class="case-heading"><h2 id="knowledge-heading">Shared Knowledge Store: a common knowledge fabric</h2></header>
  <div class="case-body">
    <p>I lead research and architecture for Shared Knowledge Store (SKS), a reusable knowledge and context foundation for Oracle AI applications. The goal is to give agents a connected understanding of an organization: its documentation, systems, incidents, dependencies and ownership, with evidence they can trace back to the source.</p>
    <p>This greenfield work brings application teams around a common foundation instead of rebuilding knowledge pipelines for every assistant. It spans evidence for site reliability engineering (SRE) and support agents, multimodal knowledge for media applications, and research on a semantic layer linking structured records with documents.</p>
    <p>The scientific challenge is assembling the right context as a task evolves: following relationships across sources, resolving ambiguity and balancing relevance, freshness and trust. I connect that research agenda with architecture decisions, evaluation and product integration.</p>

<article class="case-study" id="model-adaptation" aria-labelledby="adaptation-heading">
  <header class="case-heading"><h2 id="adaptation-heading">Model adaptation &amp; post-training</h2></header>
  <div class="case-body">
    <p>My research develops specialized model behavior through post-training: adapting pretrained models for a task or domain. It includes first-author work on fine-tuning language models for consistent responses across languages and co-first-author work on training enterprise ranking models with difficult examples.</p>
    <p>I connect training-data choices, controlled experiments and error analysis with the decisions teams make about model behavior in applications. The objective is useful specialization that holds up across languages, sources and tasks.</p>

<article class="case-study" id="database-ai" aria-labelledby="database-heading">
  <header class="case-heading"><h2 id="database-heading">Oracle AI Database 26ai</h2></header>
  <div class="case-body">
    <p>Enterprise AI needs to connect what a business knows with what its applications can do. My work in the Oracle AI Database 26ai ecosystem addresses that connection through shared knowledge architecture, semantic context and the science of selecting evidence for agents.</p>
    <p>My architecture work connects systems through shared business concepts and relationships, examining how ontologies and knowledge graphs can make evidence usable across applications. Oracle's database teams provide the underlying search, graph and agent-execution capabilities; I focus on the meaning, evidence and model behavior that applications build on.</p>

<article class="case-study" id="support-agents" aria-labelledby="support-heading">
  <header class="case-heading"><h2 id="support-heading">Oracle Support &amp; Agent Hub</h2></header>
  <div class="case-body">
    <p>I lead applied-science work that helps support agents move from answering questions to investigating and progressing service requests. The work connects ticket history, product knowledge, multi-turn reasoning and tool use across triage, incident investigation and resolution workflows.</p>
    <p>I work with support and engineering teams to turn failures into research priorities and integration decisions. My Agent Hub architecture work includes semantic routing—using a request's meaning and the task's state to select an agent, tool or knowledge source—along with shared context and memory. Records of agent decisions and tool results help us analyze failures and refine the workflow.</p>

<article class="case-study" id="multimodal-search" aria-labelledby="media-search-heading">
  <header class="case-heading"><h2 id="media-search-heading">Video, media search &amp; creative AI</h2></header>
  <div class="case-body">
    <p>I lead work that makes documents, images and video usable as a connected knowledge source. A media workflow may need a precise moment within a long video, a visual concept that has no useful caption, or evidence distributed across several assets.</p>
    <p>My creative-media research includes evaluating how a written story becomes a sequence of visual frames: whether events remain in order, characters stay consistent and the result preserves the intended narrative. Current directions include scene editing and character- and storyline-aware search. Earlier at Jio Platforms, I led video-analytics work on detection, tracking and model integration.</p>

  <header class="case-heading"><h2 id="evaluation-heading">Agent &amp; multimodal evaluation</h2></header>
  <div class="case-body">
    <p>I lead evaluation as a research and product discipline: deciding what successful behavior means, where an agent can fail and what evidence a team needs before changing a model or workflow. The scope spans long-horizon support tasks, cloud-console actions and creative-media quality.</p>
