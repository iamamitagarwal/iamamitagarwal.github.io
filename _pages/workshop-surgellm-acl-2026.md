I am a lead organizer of SURGeLLM, which explores how language models understand, retrieve and generate information from structured data such as tables and graphs.

SURGeLLM was held at ACL 2026 on July 3 in San Diego. The workshop brought together natural language processing, information retrieval, data management and visualization researchers. Its topics include understanding tables, querying structured data in natural language, building agents that use that data and evaluating their reliability. See the [official workshop site](https://surgellm.github.io/acl2026/) for its program and accepted papers.

## Related research

My coauthored paper [Can LLMs Narrate Tabular Data?](/publications/2025-can-llms-narrate-tabular-data-an-evaluation-framework-for-natural-language-representations-of-text-to-sql-system-outputs/) evaluates how language models describe the results of database queries. My [enterprise retrieval work](/projects/enterprise-retrieval/) studies ranking for specialized knowledge.
