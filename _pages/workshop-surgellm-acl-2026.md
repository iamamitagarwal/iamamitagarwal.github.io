I am Amit Agarwal, an applied scientist at Oracle AI and a lead organizer of SURGeLLM. This page connects the workshop’s research themes with my related work.

SURGeLLM was held at ACL 2026 on July 3 in San Diego. The workshop brought together NLP, information retrieval, data management, and visualization researchers around structure-aware understanding, retrieval, generation, and evaluation. Its stated topics include tables, natural-language interfaces to structured data, structured-data agents, and reliable evaluation. See the [official workshop site](https://surgellm.github.io/acl2026/) for its program and accepted papers.

## Related research

My coauthored paper [Can LLMs Narrate Tabular Data?](/publications/2025-can-llms-narrate-tabular-data-an-evaluation-framework-for-natural-language-representations-of-text-to-sql-system-outputs/) evaluates natural-language descriptions of Text-to-SQL outputs. My [enterprise retrieval story](/projects/enterprise-retrieval/) covers ranking for domain-specific search.
