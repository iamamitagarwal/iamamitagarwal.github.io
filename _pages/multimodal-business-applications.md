Business workflows depend on more than plain text. An answer may require a document, an image, a table and the context that makes each relevant. I lead applied-science work connecting those forms of evidence with retrieval-grounded applications, working across science and product engineering.

## Fusion: from model evaluation to application behavior

My Fusion work includes multimodal retrieval demonstrations across images, audio and video, and collaboration with application engineering on how models and retrieval behave inside business workflows. My earlier contributions included the science behind an image-to-text capability deployed in production.

I work with science and application engineering teams to evaluate multimodal retrieval and translate findings into integration requirements. The practical questions include how to represent image-rich documents, preserve supporting evidence and citations, and investigate differences between a benchmark result and the application's behavior.

This work brings research comparisons into engineering discussions: which model or representation fits the task, what information is lost during parsing, and how quality and latency affect the integration. Reviews with science leadership and application teams help connect those questions with delivery priorities.

## Search and semantic context around Oracle AI Database 26ai

[Oracle AI Vector Search](https://docs.oracle.com/en/database/oracle/oracle-database/26/vecse/overview-ai-vector-search.html) supports semantic retrieval with embeddings stored alongside business data. My applied-science focus is the retrieval and evaluation needed to connect that capability with useful application behavior.

My current research also connects structured data and document evidence through shared knowledge infrastructure and semantic context. The question is how an agent identifies the right source, interprets the evidence and recognizes when it needs clarification. This connects my work on retrieval, ranking and multilingual consistency with business-application requirements.

## Working across science and engineering

The collaboration runs from research demonstrations and controlled comparisons through integration feedback and application-level re-evaluation. I help connect model behavior, failure analysis and the requirements that engineering teams need to act on.

The product setting matters: a plausible answer must also use the right evidence and fit the workflow. My research on [hard-negative mining](/projects/enterprise-retrieval/) and [evaluation reliability](/projects/multimodal-evaluation/) provides complementary ways to examine those decisions.
