    <h1 id="intro-heading">Amit Agarwal</h1>
    <p class="role">Senior Principal Applied Scientist · Oracle AI Foundations, Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI)</p>
    <p class="intro-summary">With 10+ years in AI research, I’ve <strong>built applied-science teams</strong> and led research, architecture, evaluation and delivery across agentic AI, multimodal understanding, search and document intelligence. At Oracle AI Foundations, I work under the guidance of <a href="https://www.cis.upenn.edu/~danroth/">Dan Roth</a> and <a href="https://blogs.oracle.com/authors/sujithravi/">Sujith Ravi</a>, bringing science, engineering and product teams together to translate research into capabilities that products can build on.</p>
    <div class="link-row"><a href="/publications/">Research</a><a href="/projects/">Selected projects</a><a href="/cv/">CV</a></div>
  </div>
  <div class="portrait-group"><div class="portrait-frame"><img class="portrait" src="/images/profile.jpeg" alt="Amit Agarwal" width="480" height="480" fetchpriority="high"></div><div class="portrait-profiles">{% include profile_links.html %}</div></div>
  <div class="home-bio">
    <p>My work spans enterprise-wide <strong>shared knowledge and context infrastructure</strong>, multimodal capabilities for <a href="/projects/multimodal-business-applications/">Oracle Fusion ERP agents</a>, and the <a href="/projects/database-ai/">Oracle AI Database 26ai ecosystem</a>. For Oracle Support and <a href="/projects/agentic-knowledge-systems/#console-ai">OCI Console AI</a>, I lead applied-science and architecture work that connects enterprise knowledge, cloud resources and operational signals to help agents answer customer questions, resolve service requests and investigate incidents, outages and alarms. I’m also designing and building a central Agent Hub across Oracle business verticals, giving teams a shared foundation for agent routing, tool discovery, context and memory so they can reuse capabilities rather than build them independently.</p>
    <p>Alongside product development, my research covers <strong>model adaptation, post-training</strong> and the evaluation of reasoning, robustness and bias. Current work also explores creative-media workflows for storyboarding, visual generation and editing, alongside a personalized leadership-coaching assistant prototype. Before Oracle, I led video analytics and document-understanding work at Jio Platforms and developed NLP, search and recommendation systems at Abzooba.</p>
    <p>My research collaborations include <a href="/publications/2025-accesseval-benchmarking-disability-bias-in-large-language-models/">AccessEval</a>, recipient of the <strong>EMNLP 2025 Social Impact Award</strong>, and work recognized as an <strong>ICML 2026 Spotlight</strong>. I’m an inventor on <strong>10 issued patents</strong> and contribute through mentoring, research collaboration and lead-organizing workshops at CVPR, ACL and EMNLP.</p>
    <p>I hold a master’s degree in Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence, with distinction, from Liverpool John Moores University. I’ve contributed to open research through <a href="https://cohere.com/research/aya">Cohere’s Aya initiative</a>, <a href="https://seacrowd.org/">SEACrowd</a> and <a href="https://commonlid.org/">CommonLID</a>, a collaboration involving Common Crawl, EleutherAI and MLCommons.</p>
  </div>
